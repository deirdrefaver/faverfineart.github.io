About Deirdre

Paintings rooted in the Southwest.

I paint acrylic on canvas, mostly southwestern images and Arizona landscapes. Each painting captures the vibrant colors and textures of the desert and the unique beauty of the lower Colorado region.

By day, I work in real estate here in Lake Havasu. In my free time, painting lets me explore the landscapes and details I love most about where we live.

Every piece is an original. You are welcome to see them in person by appointment, or reach out by email or phone.
